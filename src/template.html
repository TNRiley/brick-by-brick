The greys changed overnight

How this was counted

  • Source. Rebrickable's public database of official LEGO sets and their parts lists, downloaded . It is maintained by fans. Parts lists are often checked against the building instructions, but not always.
  • What counts as a piece. Every non-spare part in each set's first inventory. Minifigures are separate inventories on Rebrickable and are not included, so yellow heads and torsos are missing.
  • What is left out. Books, gear, service packs, Modulex (an architectural modelling system, not a toy), and parts with unknown colour. sets remain.
  • Years are release years as Rebrickable records them. Before 1958 there are few sets, so those columns are faded.
  • Sets, not sales. A set sold once and a set sold millions of times count the same. This is the palette LEGO designed with, not the palette in anyone's toy box.
  • Colour names are Rebrickable's, not LEGO's internal names. Colour families are this page's own grouping; the pipeline lists them.
  • The house behind the page is illustrative. Its colours for each era were picked by hand from colours common in that period.
